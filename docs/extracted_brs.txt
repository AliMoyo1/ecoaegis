1. Introduction
This Business Requirements Specification (BRS) defines the full set of functional, non-functional, security, and integration requirements for the SHE Management Platform an integrated, digital workflow system purpose-built to standardise, automate, and enforce compliance across all ten Safety, Health, and Environment (SHE) operational process modules.
The platform introduces structured workflow logic, role-based access controls (RBAC), live risk register synchronisation, and statutory reporting capabilities, consolidating them into a single governed environment for managing the organisation’s SHE obligations. These obligations span vendor management, community relations, emergency preparedness, incident investigation, training, and environmental assessments.
This BRS is technology independent. It defines what the system must do not how it should be technically implemented. The detailed system architecture and technical design are to be produced during the subsequent solution design phase.
1.1 Background and Problem Statement
The organisation’s SHE functions currently operates across ten discrete process areas, including vendor compliance management, community grievance resolution, emergency response, and environmental impact assessment. These processes are governed by manual workflows, dispersed documentation systems, and inconsistent escalation chains.
This operational fragmentation creates three categories of measurable risk that the platform is designed to resolve:
Risk Category 1 Compliance Gaps
Incident data, vendor records, and regulatory submissions are maintained in separate, unconnected systems.
There is no single auditable repository to confirm that all SHE obligations have been met.
Regulatory submissions can be missed or incomplete due to lack of a central tracking mechanism.
Risk Category 2 Escalation Failures
Approvals and follow-up actions depend on email chains and verbal instructions rather than enforced workflow routing.
There is no system-level mechanism to prevent approval skipping or unauthorised delegation.
Cases can stall indefinitely without automated escalation to higher authority.
Risk Category 3 Statutory Exposure
Reporting timelines to external bodies such as NSSA, EMA, and ZRP are at risk when tracked manually.
There is no automated notification system to alert responsible parties when statutory deadlines are approaching.
Missed submissions create legal and reputational exposure for the organisation.
The SHE Management Platform directly addresses these three risk categories by establishing a centralised, workflow-driven system that enforces process compliance, automates escalation, and maintains a live, cross-module Corporate Risk Register.
1.2 Objectives
1.2.1 Core System Objectives
Digitise and enforce all ten SHE operational process workflows within a single governed platform.
Maintain a live Central Risk Register that is synchronised across incident, grievance, emergency, and environmental modules in real time.
Enforce role-based workflow routing and approval chains aligned to the RACI framework defined in each process standard.
Automate statutory reporting submissions to NSSA, EMA, and Law Enforcement where regulatory timelines apply.
Provide end-to-end audit trails for every SHE processes action, approval, and closure decision.
1.2.2 Business Objectives
Eliminate unauthorised vendor site deployments through mandatory, system-issued Permit to Work (PTW) issuance and validation.
Reduce community grievance escalation into legal crises through structured intake, investigation, and resolution workflows.
Ensure zero environmental regulatory violations on new project developments by enforcing Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) completion prior to project clearance.
Improve executive visibility into corporate SHE risks through structured, timely reporting at operational, management, and Board levels.
1.2.3 Operational Objectives
Integrate with Procurement, HR, Legal, and Finance systems to support vendor onboarding, training coordination, and emergency resource provisioning.
Deliver a clean, intuitive user interface accessible to field-level SHE Champions, Officers, Managers, and executive approvers.
Support configurable notification and escalation thresholds aligned to each process module’s compliance timelines.
Maintain a corporate Learning Management System (LMS) integration to synchronise training records and competency matrices.

The platform must enforce a unified role architecture aligned to the RACI assignments defined across all ten SHE process standards. Each role maps to a distinct access tier with specific platform permissions and workflow authorities. The following roles must be implemented:

3. Core Master Data Registries
The platform database architecture must maintain the following global master registries. These registries are accessible across all ten process modules and are governed by enforced synchronisation logic meaning data written in one module automatically propagates to the relevant registry without manual intervention.
3.1 Central Risk Register
A live logistical matrix connecting environmental impacts, active workplace hazards, asset vulnerabilities, and ongoing remediation steps across all business divisions. The Risk Register is the single source of truth for corporate SHE risk status.
The Risk Register must be updated automatically when any of the following events occur:
An incident is closed in the Accident & Incident module (SHEIMI), including root cause classification and remediation status.
A community grievance is resolved or classified as an unresolved residual risk in the Grievance module (SHECCM).
An emergency response is closed out in the Emergency Response module (SHEER), with post-crisis findings recorded.
An EIA is rejected or a project is flagged as high-risk in the Environmental Impact Assessment module (SHEIA).
3.2 Key Issues SHE Tracker
A live operational log tracking audit exceptions, open non-conformances, daily reporting metrics, and closed safety observations. The tracker must be updated whenever a SHE report is approved in the Reporting module (SHER) and must support visual data display for executive dashboards.
Items in the Key Issues Tracker must be assigned an age threshold  unresolved items that exceed this threshold must automatically escalate to the SHE Manager and CRO.
3.3 Vendor Compliance Roster
A registry mapping third-party contractors against active insurance policies, certifications, risk profiles, and approved site-access permits. The roster must integrate with the Procurement ERP system and must automatically flag expired certifications or lapsed PTWs. A vendor with an expired certification or lapsed PTW must have their PTW eligibility suspended until the renewal is recorded.
3.4 Community Grievance Register
A formal case log recording all grievances received from external stakeholders. Each record must capture source channel, date received, grievance classification, assigned investigating officer, investigation status, resolution outcome, and complainant notification history. This register provides a full, auditable trail of every community interaction.
3.5 Training Records and Competency Matrix
A repository of all completed training sessions, trainee rosters, post-training competency scores, and upcoming scheduled refresher courses. This registry must synchronise bidirectionally with the corporate Learning Management System (LMS) pushing completion data when a session is closed and pulling LMS data when assessing training gaps.

5.1  Module: Vendor SHE Compliance Management (SHECMV)
Functional Objective: Standardise the structural assessment, contract governance, monitoring, and closure of contractor-executed on-site works. The module must ensure zero unauthorised vendor site deployments and 100% PTW coverage for all high-risk tasks.
5.2 Module: Community Complaints Management (SHECCM)
Functional Objective: Provide a structured, accountable process for receiving, investigating, and resolving community grievances. Ensure complainant notification and maintain a formal, auditable case record for every grievance received.
5.3 Module: External Communication for Environmental & Social Concerns (SHE EC&SC)
Functional Objective: Govern the identification, internal approval, and controlled distribution of public-facing communications regarding environmental and social impacts. Ensure 100% HOD vetting before any external release.
5.4 Module: Emergency Preparedness & Response Planning (SHEEPRP)
Functional Objective: Systematise the creation, resource mapping, testing, and continuous improvement of the corporate Disaster Management Response Plan. Enforce timely mock drill execution and ensure all emergency resources are formally provisioned through the system.
5.5 Module: Accident & Incident Reporting and Investigation (SHEIMI)
Functional Objective: Enforce automated tracking, thorough investigation routing, compliance escalation, and statutory closure for all workplace accidents and safety incidents. Ensure 100% root-cause tracing for all critical losses.
5.6 Module: Annual Workplan Management (SHEAWPM)
Functional Objective: Govern the full lifecycle of the annual corporate SHE operational plan from initial baseline assessment through committee review, executive approval, and real-time task tracking throughout the fiscal year.
5.7 Module: Live Emergency Response (SHEER)
Functional Objective: Execute real-time communications, executive strategic direction, staff relocation coordination, site clearance certification, and statutory notifications during an active crisis event. Ensure no time-critical step is missed through enforced workflow routing.
5.8 Module: SHE Business Reporting (SHER)
Functional Objective: Automate the creation, multi-tiered review, and action tracking for periodic Board, management, and operational safety performance reports. Ensure on-time submissions and live visibility in the Key Issues Tracker.
5.9 Module: Environmental & Social Awareness Training (SHET&A)
Functional Objective: Coordinate training needs assessments, external vendor onboarding, course delivery, and feedback capture for environmental and safety awareness programmes. Maintain continuous alignment between training content and recent incident trends.
5.10 Module: Environmental Impact Assessment for New Projects (SHEIA)
Functional Objective: Standardise compliance logic for screening, commissioning, evaluating, and submitting EIAs for all engineering and infrastructure initiatives. Enforce zero environmental regulatory violations and 100% onboarding of EMA-accredited consultants.

11.2  Risk Scoring and Classification Logic
The platform must implement the following scoring and classification logic, consistent with the existing register methodology:
Inherent Risk Score = Likelihood (1–5) × Impact (1–5). Maximum inherent score = 25.
Residual Risk Score = Inherent Risk Score ÷ Control Effectiveness (1–5). A higher control effectiveness rating reduces the residual score.
Risk Category must be selectable from the following values: Operational, Financial, Regulatory, Strategic.
Risk Direction must be selectable from: Increasing (↑), Stable (→), Decreasing (↓). This field supports executive trend dashboards.
Risks with an Aggregate Residual Score ≥ 12 must be automatically flagged as High Priority and escalated to the CRO dashboard.
Risks with an Aggregate Residual Score of 8–11 are classified as Medium Priority and must appear on the SHE Manager review queue.
Risks with an Aggregate Residual Score ≤ 7 are classified as Low Priority and are managed at the operational level.
